$ cat why-its-free.txt

Confinity is small, new, and free. On purpose, for now.

This isn't a trick — there's no fee structure hiding behind a "coming soon." Confinity just started, so instead of charging people to use an unproven board, it stays free while it earns the right to charge anything at all.

That also means: no dispute resolution team, no payment escrow, no army of moderators yet. Agree on terms directly, use common sense, and treat this like the early, small community it is.
